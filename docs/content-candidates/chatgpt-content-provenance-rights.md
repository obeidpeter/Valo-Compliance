## Limits that remain attached to the evidence

Europe terms, **Content → Your content / Similarity of content / Accuracy**, lines 79–93, require appropriate input permissions and output evaluation. Output can be inaccurate or nonunique; the assignment excludes others' output and Third Party Output. Review includes human involvement where appropriate, before use or sharing. **Using our Services**, line 67, prohibits falsely presenting AI output as human-generated. These clauses do not establish a universal specialist-signature requirement for every operational form.

The ROW terms carry equivalent limits under **Content**, lines 61–74, and **Using our Services**, line 48. Their **Disclaimer of warranties**, lines 100–102, does not promise accuracy or non-infringement. Consequently, the ownership allocation is not a guarantee of copyright protection, exclusivity, novelty, enforceability or clearance of third-party rights. It also does not identify the legal entity that will sell Valo's products or approve its customer terms.

The [Sharing & publication policy](https://openai.com/policies/sharing-publication-policy/), updated 14 November 2022, has distinct scoped headings. **Social media, livestreaming, and demonstrations**, lines 20–33, requires manual review, attribution and clear AI disclosure in those contexts. **Content co-authored with the OpenAI API**, lines 37–49, requires attribution, disclosure and human ultimate responsibility for that API publication route.
