# Valo operational template candidates

Prepared: 13 November 2026. Version: candidate-1.

These are seven original prose and field-layout candidates written for the user's Valo build brief. IDs, titles, release and access classes were checked against `content_inventory.json`. They are content inputs for the application, not application code and not published catalogue items.

**Current status: DRAFT — HELD pending EDP.** No factual-review, challenge-review, rights clearance, export inspection or publication is claimed. The drafting provenance is this AI-assisted authoring task; provider/account terms and reuse rights still require retained evidence. The forms deliberately make no assertions about statutory duties, prescribed retention periods, tax treatment, professional certification or regulatory approval. The final risk class depends on the actual released content and use; a form's title alone does not establish low risk.

Square brackets mark fields to complete. An empty field means not entered; it must not silently become zero, false, approved or not applicable. Dates should include the year. Record a timezone when a time matters. Use one currency per monetary total. Do not place confidential customer details, passwords, bank-card information or identity documents in these forms. In the R1 product, record references to supporting material rather than uploading evidence files.

The owner may adapt a candidate before its full EDP review. Any adapted version needs its own content hash, reviews, rights record, validation and export evidence. An internal manager review field within a business form is an operational record; it is not Valo publication approval or specialist certification.

## Editorial and export handoff checklist

Each candidate needs its own retained evidence before it contributes to catalogue counts:

1. Confirm the ID/title and actual content class/risk within the owner-authorised policy.
2. Retain creation provenance and actual provider/account use-rights evidence. Do not mark rights cleared merely because this text is original.
3. Run separate factual and challenge reviews against the exact candidate. Review scope, missing fields, misleading business claims, sensitive-data collection and any accidental legal assertion.
4. Validate schema, empty-versus-zero behaviour, table structure, arithmetic where relevant, revision binding and disclosures.
5. Render real DOCX and PDF versions. Inspect table wrapping, page breaks, repeated headings, empty writing space, currency glyphs and accessibility/readability. These prose layouts are not export evidence.
6. Bind content, policy, rights, reviews and tests in the immutable manifest and use the independently permissioned publisher.
7. Only after actual reviews pass, display the required truthful release disclosure: “AI drafted and reviewed; not reviewed by a Nigerian legal, tax or sector specialist.” Do not apply that past-tense claim to these still-unreviewed candidates.
8. Premium access classes remain subject to actual entitlement and owner-accepted commercial licence/pricing/refund requirements before sales.
