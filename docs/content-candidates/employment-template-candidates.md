## Internal status, scope and preparation controls

**All five candidates are DRAFT / HELD pending enhanced EDP-001–012 checks.** No independent factual/challenge review, legal suitability, professional review, rights clearance, source currentness, export inspection or publication is claimed. The full bodies below are intended to become usable documents after their factual schedules and applicable legal controls are completed. An empty legal or commercial variable is not permission to select a convenient default.

Exact R1 classifications: TPL-16 Employment contract — Premium, Guided; TPL-17 Offer letter — Premium, Guided; TPL-26 Employee handbook — Premium, Download; TPL-27 Leave policy — Premium, Download; TPL-28 Disciplinary procedure — Premium, Download. None is an authorised R1 customer AI legal-drafting task. Internal editorial AI may prepare and review them through EDP; customer use must stay within the released, bounded template and guided fields.

These candidates concern adult employment performed in Nigeria for a private business. They are not contractor agreements. Public-service appointments, apprenticeships, employment of children or young persons, domestic service, recruitment for work abroad, maritime/aviation work, regulated roles and unresolved collective arrangements require separately verified scope. A job title or a checkbox does not establish whether a person falls within a statutory definition. Unresolved coverage holds the affected legal provision; it must not become an exemption.

### Common applicability record — internal, not a customer clause

Record the employer's identity, sector, locations and actual employee count; the person's age category, actual duties, work location and hours; employment versus independent-contractor facts; fixed-term/indefinite basis; relevant collective agreement, award or sector rule; prior continuous service; applicable employment-law category; payroll, pension and other benefit scope; and the sources/effective periods supporting each conclusion. Minimise identity data: this record does not require an identity-document image or a person's medical history.

Verify each material statutory minimum or restriction against the operative source and the employee's coverage before permitting the corresponding field. Pay, deductions, notice, working time, leave, maternity-related arrangements, benefits, suspension, dismissal and redundancy must not be inferred from generic model knowledge. Keep contractual improvements distinct from legal minima. This file intentionally supplies no default statutory periods, wage floor, pension rate or leave quantity.

The only proposed customer bodies are the sections labelled **Customer document**. Internal controls, source notes and review targets must not enter completed employment documents. Brackets are variables; resolve required variables before ready-to-use export, otherwise mark the whole output visibly incomplete. Do not invent an agreement, signature, approval, qualification or completion event.

When the final manifest truthfully supports it, the template detail must display the required AI drafting/review disclosure and absence of professional legal review. At this draft stage, the accurate description is “AI drafted; review pending; no professional legal review.”

## TPL-16 — Employment contract

R1 · Premium · Guided · Enhanced employment/legal-content checks required

### Guided field and scope controls

Required facts: employer and employee legal identities and addresses, engagement place/date, start date and any recognised earlier service, actual role/duties, work location, indefinite/fixed-term basis and end date where relevant, gross pay and calculation/payment cycle, working hours/rest arrangements, verified leave/benefit schedule, notice arrangements, applicable policies and contact routes. The employer's employee count is an applicability input, not a reason to automatically disable all benefits.

A probation clause is **off by default**. If requested, require an expressly agreed, scope-checked period, review process and lawful notice/pay treatment; do not add automatic extension or dismissal without notice. Any proposed restrictive covenant, broad intellectual-property assignment, training-cost repayment, deduction, employment bond or arbitration clause needs its own verified module and cannot be generated as free text.

The guided flow must reconcile this contract with an existing offer and more favourable applicable terms. Missing pay, leave or notice figures cannot be hidden behind “company policy” unless the precise accessible policy supplies the validated terms. Validate arithmetic, effective dates, schedule completeness and document precedence. No employee-specific legal opinion is generated.

## TPL-28 — Disciplinary procedure

R1 · Premium · Download · Enhanced employment/legal-content checks required

### Preparation controls

This is a proposed fair internal procedure, not a claim that each step is a statutory rule for every employee. Identify covered groups, existing contracts/collective terms, decision authority, representation provisions and review routes. The process must not create a power to suspend without pay, deduct wages, dismiss summarily or impose financial penalties. Such actions require an independently established lawful basis and situation-specific checks. A blanket sanction table or automatic “three strikes” rule is excluded.

### Customer document

# DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE

[Business name] · Version [ ] · Effective date [ ]  
Covered employees [ ] · Policy owner [ ]

### 1. Purpose and principles

This procedure provides a way to consider a workplace concern fairly and keep a clear record. An allegation is not a finding. The business will consider the relevant evidence, explain the concern and give the employee an appropriate opportunity to respond before reaching a disciplinary decision. The process will be applied consistently while allowing for the actual circumstances and applicable terms.

Performance capability, ill health, a grievance and alleged misconduct may need different processes. We will identify the issue before choosing a procedure. Raising a concern honestly or exercising an applicable right is not itself misconduct. No outcome is automatic because a complaint was made, a meeting was missed or an employee declined to sign a note.

### 2. Raising and assessing a concern

Report the concern to [contact] or [alternative where the first person is involved]. Record the relevant events, dates and available supporting references without exaggeration. Limit personal information to what is needed.

[Responsible person] will consider whether immediate support, informal clarification, a different process or a formal investigation is appropriate. An informal discussion will not be represented later as a formal warning unless a formal decision was actually made and communicated through the appropriate process.

### 3. Immediate arrangements

If there is an immediate safety, safeguarding, security or evidence-preservation concern, a responsible person will consider proportionate interim arrangements. They will record the reason, scope, duration/review point and effect on duties and pay, after checking the applicable authority and terms. An interim measure is not a finding of guilt.

This procedure does not itself authorise unpaid suspension, exclusion indefinitely, confiscation of personal devices or interference with a lawful report. Any proposed suspension or other material restriction needs its own lawful basis and review.

### 4. Investigation

An investigator will be appointed with regard to conflicts of interest. Where practicable, the investigator and final decision-maker will be different people. The investigation will identify both supporting and contrary evidence and record unresolved gaps. Witness accounts will be labelled as accounts rather than established facts.

Information will be shared only as needed for a fair process and other applicable obligations. Absolute confidentiality will not be promised. Evidence must not be altered, fabricated or obtained through unauthorised account access.

### 5. Notice and response

If a formal response is needed, the employee will receive a clear description of the concern, the material to be considered subject to appropriate protections, the possible type of outcome, the meeting details and the applicable representation/support arrangements. The employee will have reasonable preparation time assessed in light of the circumstances and any binding requirements; no arbitrary universal deadline is imposed here.

The employee may provide an explanation, identify relevant evidence, correct inaccuracies and raise concerns about the process or a conflict of interest. Requests for appropriate adjustments, interpretation or a rearranged meeting will be considered. If the employee cannot attend, the business will clarify the reason and consider an appropriate alternative before deciding how to proceed. Silence is not treated as an admission.

### 6. Meeting and decision

The decision-maker will explain the issues, consider the response and ask relevant questions. A note will be kept, with disputed wording recorded rather than forcing agreement. Further material information may require additional investigation or an opportunity to respond before a decision.

The decision will distinguish findings from unresolved allegations and state the evidence and reasons relied on. Possible outcomes include no disciplinary action, clarification/support, an improvement arrangement or an appropriately authorised warning. Any more serious action will be considered only after checking the applicable contract, law, procedure and circumstances. This policy does not pre-authorise dismissal, a wage deduction, a fine or forfeiture of earned benefits.

Where an improvement or warning is issued, the notice will explain the issue, expected change, support, review arrangement and any period for which it will be considered under the applicable terms. No warning period is invented or applied retrospectively.

### 7. Communication and review

The employee will receive the written outcome, its reasons, effective date where relevant and the route for review or appeal. The communication will identify [review contact] and the applicable response period [validated policy/contractual arrangement]. If additional time is reasonably needed, the request will be considered; this is not presented as a statutory appeal deadline unless verified as such.

A reviewer will consider the stated grounds, relevant record and any new material information. Where practicable, the reviewer will not be the original decision-maker. The review outcome and reasons will be communicated and retained. Using this process does not waive an available court, regulator, representative or other external route.

### 8. Records and follow-up

The case record will contain the concern, scope, evidence references, notices, response, decision, review and any corrective action. Access and retention will follow [employee-data arrangement/reference]. Unsubstantiated allegations will not be relabelled as proven facts in later records.

Follow-up will check whether the agreed action or support occurred and whether the record needs correction. An employee's acknowledgement of receiving a decision is not an admission, agreement with it or waiver of a right to review.
