## TPL-12 — Payment reminder letter

R1 · Free · Guided + customer AI · One of the four authorised R1 customer AI drafting workflows

**Purpose:** Ask about an amount that the business's checked records show as outstanding. This letter is not a receipt, a legal demand or proof that a debt is undisputed or enforceable.

### Information to confirm before preparing the letter

- Sender business name: [ ]
- Customer name and intended contact: [ ]
- Letter date and date used to assess payment timing: [actual date]
- Invoice reference and issue date: [ ]
- Currency: [ ]
- Original invoice amount: [ ]
- Confirmed payments allocated to this invoice: [ ]
- Confirmed credits or other agreed reductions: [ ]
- Other agreed adjustment, if any, with an explanation and reference: [ ]
- Recorded balance: [ ]
- Balance checked on: [date and, if relevant, time/timezone]
- Payment due date, **only if established**: [ ]; basis/reference: [ ]
- Disputed amount or issue, if any: [ ]
- Existing payment arrangement or customer response, if any: [ ]
- Verified reply channel: [ ]
- Verified payment instructions, if the sender chooses to include them: [ ]; verification basis/date: [ ]

Recorded balance = invoice amount − confirmed allocated payments − confirmed credits/reductions + separately established additional adjustments. Do not infer interest, penalties or tax. Count the same transaction once. Do not combine currencies without an explicitly supplied and agreed conversion basis.

### Letter

**[Sender business name]**  
[Sender contact detail chosen for this correspondence]  
[Letter date]

To: [Customer name / agreed contact]

**Subject: Payment enquiry — invoice [invoice reference]**

Dear [Customer/contact name],

We are following up on invoice [invoice reference], dated [invoice issue date], for [brief description of the supplied goods or work].

As of [balance-check date], our records show an original invoice amount of [currency and amount], payments of [currency and confirmed allocated payments] and confirmed credits or reductions of [currency and amount, or “none recorded”].

[Include only when separately established: An additional agreed adjustment of [currency and signed amount] relates to [description and reference] and is included in the balance.]

After those recorded payments, credits and any separately stated adjustment, the remaining recorded balance is [currency and balance].

[Insert one appropriate timing paragraph below.]

[Insert the dispute or payment-arrangement paragraph below only where relevant.]

If you have already paid, please reply with the payment date, amount and a useful transaction reference so we can check our records. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unrelated bank information.

[Optional, only where verified: For payment, please use the following instructions: [verified instructions].]

Please contact [reply contact/channel] if there is a question about the invoice or if any part of our record is incorrect.

Thank you,

[Sender name]  
[Role, if supplied]  
[Business name]

### Choose the timing paragraph that matches the supplied facts

- **Established due date has passed:** “The agreed payment date recorded for this invoice was [date]. Please let us know the expected payment date, or raise any issue that needs resolving.”
- **Established due date is the letter date:** “The payment date recorded for this invoice is today, [date]. Please let us know if there is a question about the payment or our records.”
- **Established due date is still ahead:** “The payment date recorded for this invoice is [date]. This is a reminder ahead of that date.”
- **No established payment date:** “Please let us know when we can expect payment, or whether we need to clarify the payment arrangement.”

Do not call an invoice overdue where the due date is missing, disputed or still ahead. A user-chosen follow-up date is not a statutory deadline.

### Optional paragraph for a dispute or arrangement

- **Dispute:** “We understand that [specific issue] is still being discussed. The amount currently identified as disputed is [currency and amount, if known]. Please help us resolve [specific question]. This message does not treat that issue as settled.”
- **Existing arrangement:** “Our recorded arrangement is [actual agreed arrangement]. Please let us know if our understanding is incorrect or if you need to discuss it.”

### Guided input and validation requirements

Required to produce a usable completed letter: sender, customer, actual letter date, invoice reference, currency, invoice amount, checked payment/credit facts, balance-check date and reply channel. Compare the established due date with the actual letter date, not a guessed or hidden date. Any additional adjustment must appear with its signed amount, explanation and reference in both the reconciliation and the letter; no hidden adjustment is permitted. A missing amount must not become zero. A customer assertion that payment was sent must remain distinct from reconciled payment records.

The editor must calculate the balance deterministically, flag duplicate payment references and resolve conflicting figures before acceptance. If the balance is zero, offer an account-status clarification instead of a demand for payment. If negative, flag a possible excess payment for reconciliation; do not initiate or promise a refund. The user must review the draft before accepting it. The AI workflow has no email, collection, payment or legal-filing action.

**Fictional example, not a default:** Demo Seller A issued invoice DEMO-1201 for NGN25,000.00. One confirmed payment of NGN10,000.00 and one confirmed credit of NGN2,000.00 leave NGN13,000.00. No due date was supplied, so the neutral timing paragraph applies. This example creates no actual invoice, payment or reminder.
