## TPL-25 — Board resolution

### Authoring variables and conditions

Required before the document can describe an adopted resolution: exact company name and company type; registration identifier if known; meeting date/time/place or actual remote arrangements; meeting reference; current articles/version; matter-specific authority reference; actual notice/attendance/entitlement and quorum records; conflicts treatment; exact resolution text; actual voting record; recorded outcome; minute recorder and record date. Do not infer authority from a title, use an NGO constitution as company articles, or create an attendance, consent, vote or signature.

The customer body is for an identified Nigerian **company board meeting**. An incorporated trustee, association, partnership, sole proprietor or body governed under another framework must use a form adapted to its actual governing instrument and powers; this body does not claim their authority. Matters reserved to members or requiring another approval are not made effective by a board record. Written resolutions are outside this meeting form; CAMA s.289(8) must not be substituted with a majority checkbox.

Outcome values: **Proposed / Deferred / Not adopted / Recorded as adopted subject to stated conditions**. “Recorded as adopted” requires a completed actual vote record and the applicable decision rule. No default quorum, majority, casting vote or notice period is supplied. Record any actual signature only after the person acts; generating this document is not signing or certifying a true copy. No synthetic completed example is necessary for this premium download form.

## TPL-33 — Health and safety checklist

### Authoring variables and conditions

Original general workplace observation/action record; no statutory inspection, equipment certification, quantified risk method, medical diagnosis or comprehensive safety assessment is claimed. Set the site/activity, inspection boundaries, responsible person and applicable local procedures before use. Task-specific or regulated technical inspections must be recorded by reference to the actual competent work, not simulated by this checklist. GS-02 supports the distinction between a business record and the official oversight routes.

Status choices per item: **Observed acceptable against named criterion / Action needed / Not checked / Not applicable with reason**. An unexamined row cannot default to acceptable. Each “action needed” links to an action owner and control; an “acceptable” observation must not imply all future conditions are safe. Avoid numerical risk scores unless an identified adopted method supplies their meaning.

## TPL-36 — Product recall plan

### Authoring variables and conditions

Scope: food and food products manufactured, packed or distributed by the identified business, with site/product applicability established. It is not a ready-made pharmaceutical, medical-device, veterinary, cosmetics or other product-sector recall procedure. A broader catalogue title is preserved while the body clearly states this edition's food scope. GS-03 §32/32.1 and, where applicable, GS-05 §8 are narrow source anchors.

Required setup: products/sites, accountable coordinator and deputy, decision authority, verified authority contact route, traceability locations, communication authority, stock-control arrangements and current procedure references. Actual incident records start empty. Dates are user-entered; no fixed legal reporting interval, recall class, disposal method, retention period or product-release permission is generated. A mock exercise stays labelled throughout and cannot initiate real customer messages.

Quantity reconciliation uses one explicit unit and non-overlapping physical locations at a stated cutoff. Initially distributed units may later move into returned custody; never add those two historical figures as though they were separate units. Counts of contacted recipients are distinct from physical product quantities. The plan remains distinct from an activated recall; only actual business/authority decisions change event status.
