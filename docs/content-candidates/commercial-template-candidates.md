## TPL-13 — Service agreement

R1 · Premium · Guided

### Agreement details and completion check

This agreement is between **[Customer legal name, entity type, registration reference if applicable, address]** (Customer) and **[Provider legal name, entity type, registration reference if applicable, address]** (Provider). Agreement reference: [ ]. Date of agreement: [ ]. Start date: [ ]. End date or completion event: [ ]. Each signatory's authority reference: [ ].

The parties must complete the schedules and the governing-law/venue entry before signing. This form concerns an ordinary commercial service engagement. It does not authorise regulated professional services, employment, consumer-rights exclusions, a deed, a land transaction or processing of personal data merely because those activities are mentioned in a service description. Any such element needs a suitable, separately settled arrangement.

### 1. Services and responsibilities

The Provider will perform the services and supply the deliverables described in Schedule A with reasonable care and skill, using personnel and resources suitable for the agreed work. The Provider will follow the agreed requirements and promptly identify a conflict, missing instruction or dependency that may affect delivery. It will not substitute a materially different service without the Customer's written agreement.

The Customer will provide the information, access and decisions allocated to it in Schedule A, through its named contact. Each party will promptly tell the other if information it supplied is materially inaccurate or incomplete. The Provider must not treat an unresolved assumption as an approved instruction. A delay caused by a dependency will be recorded with its effect and a proposed revised plan; dates and fees do not change automatically.

### 2. Delivery, review and correction

The Provider will submit each deliverable with its reference and the agreed acceptance criteria. Within the review period in Schedule A, the Customer will either confirm acceptance in writing or describe the material differences from those criteria. The Provider will correct an identified failure to meet the agreed requirements without an additional fee and resubmit it within the correction period or a written revised timetable. A new or changed requirement follows clause 3.

Silence, a test, partial use or an invoice is not automatic acceptance. The parties will record accepted parts and disputed parts separately. A review period does not remove rights concerning a defect that could not reasonably be identified in that review or any right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

### 3. Changes

Either party may request a change. Before changed work begins, the parties' authorised contacts must agree a written change record stating the revised work, price, timing, dependencies and acceptance criteria. The Provider may continue unaffected work while a change is considered. An informal discussion does not authorise extra charges. If continuing unchanged work would create a safety, security or unlawful-processing concern, the parties will pause the affected work and agree a safe response.

### 4. Fees, expenses and payment

The Customer will pay the fees against the milestones or periods in Schedule B after receiving an invoice identifying the agreement, work and calculation. Only expenses approved in writing in advance are chargeable, up to the recorded limit and with supporting references. An invoice dispute must identify the amount and reason; the parties will seek to resolve it promptly and pay the undisputed amount by its agreed due date. No interest, penalty, tax rate or currency conversion is supplied by this form.

Schedule B must state whether prices include or exclude each applicable tax and any verified withholding arrangement. If an amount is withheld under an applicable requirement, the payer will identify the basis and provide the associated evidence when available. Neither party will invent a rate or describe a deduction as legally required without confirming its basis for this transaction.

### 5. Materials and deliverable rights

Each party retains rights it already held in materials supplied to the engagement. It permits the other to use those materials only as needed for this agreement and confirms that it has authority for that use. The Provider will disclose proposed third-party materials and their restrictions before incorporating them; approval of a deliverable does not silently accept an undisclosed third-party licence.

Schedule C must identify the new deliverables and select one rights arrangement. Under **Licence**, on payment of the agreed fee for the covered deliverable the Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, worldwide, continuing licence to use, copy and adapt it for the stated purpose, including through service providers bound to protect it. Under **Assignment**, on payment of the agreed fee for the covered deliverable the Provider assigns its transferable copyright in that specifically identified new deliverable to the Customer; background and third-party materials remain excluded. For either arrangement, the Provider grants a non-exclusive, worldwide, continuing licence in embedded background material to the extent it controls the permissions needed for that stated use. The Provider will not include material it cannot license on that basis without prior written agreement. No clause transfers an author's entire future work or claims to transfer rights the Provider does not hold.

### 6. Confidentiality, access and data

Each party will protect the other's non-public information identified as confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential, use it only for the engagement, and disclose it only to people who need it and are bound to protect it. This does not cover information the recipient can show was already lawfully known, became public without breach, was independently developed or was lawfully obtained without restriction. A legally required disclosure may be made to the extent required, with prior notice where permitted. Nothing prevents a lawful report to a competent authority.

These confidentiality duties apply during the engagement and for the protection period after its end stated in Schedule D. Restricted copies retained under an agreed, verified retention arrangement remain protected while retained.

Access is limited to the agreed systems and tasks. Neither party may upload the other's confidential information or personal data to an unapproved AI or external service. If the work entails processing personal data for the other party, the affected processing must not begin until the parties settle their roles, instructions, security, approved locations/subprocessors and the appropriate separate written terms. A security or confidentiality incident affecting the engagement must be reported promptly to the named contact with known facts and containment steps; neither party may conceal uncertainty or promise a statutory notification outcome through this clause.

### 7. Subcontracting and relationship

The Provider may use a subcontractor for material work only with the Customer's prior written consent, which will identify the work and relevant access. The Provider remains responsible for its contractual performance. Neither party can bind the other to a third-party obligation without express written authority. This agreement records a service arrangement; its label does not determine any person's legal employment or tax status.

### 8. Ending the engagement

Either party may end the engagement for convenience on the notice period in Schedule D. For a material breach capable of remedy, the affected party will give written particulars and the remedy period in Schedule D; it may terminate the affected engagement if the breach remains unremedied. A party may suspend the affected work where continuing would be unlawful or create an immediate material safety or security risk, explaining the scope and reason as soon as practicable. This is not permission to suspend unrelated work or retain the other's property as leverage.

On ending, the Provider will provide completed work and an orderly handover of work paid for or payable under the agreed termination calculation. The Customer will pay for conforming work actually performed and approved non-cancellable commitments expressly identified in Schedule D, less amounts already paid. The Provider will refund the unearned balance of any advance. There is no automatic forfeiture or charge for all future work. The parties will reconcile disputed amounts separately and return or securely dispose of materials under clause 6 and any applicable separate retention arrangement.

The termination record will identify each completed or incomplete work handed over, its allocated settlement amount, the Schedule C rights arrangement and any limitations on its intended use. Payment of that allocated amount satisfies clause 5's payment condition for the identified work; no fee for unperformed future work is required. Incomplete work must remain labelled as such. Rights already obtained in paid-for work are not revoked merely because the engagement ends.

### 9. Responsibility, disruption and disputes

Each party remains responsible for its own breach and the acts of people for whom it is contractually responsible. This form imposes no blanket exclusion, automatic indemnity or liability cap. Any negotiated limitation needs its own explicit, reviewed wording; rights that cannot lawfully be excluded remain unaffected.

A party affected by an event outside its reasonable control will promptly explain the effect, take reasonable steps to reduce disruption and propose a recovery plan. Payment for work already properly performed remains due. The parties may end affected unperformed work if disruption continues beyond the period in Schedule D, with the same fair reconciliation above.

The named contacts will first try to resolve a dispute, escalating to the named decision-makers after the period in Schedule D. This agreement is governed by the applicable laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and **[identified State/FCT and relevant state-law selection]**. The parties select the courts of competent jurisdiction in **[agreed location, subject to mandatory jurisdiction rules]**. This clause does not create jurisdiction a court does not have or prevent urgent relief or a lawful regulatory complaint.

### 10. Notices and agreement record

Contract notices must identify this agreement and be delivered to the postal or email notice addresses in Schedule D, with a reliable record of receipt. An automatic delivery message alone does not settle a disputed receipt. A changed address must be notified. This notice arrangement does not purport to replace court-service requirements.

This agreement and its signed schedules record the parties' agreement about this engagement. A signed change record prevails only for the change it identifies; otherwise the agreement prevails over a conflicting schedule unless the schedule expressly identifies the clause it changes. Purchase-order boilerplate is not incorporated merely by referencing an order. Amendments must be agreed in writing by authorised representatives. Nothing here excludes liability for fraud or a right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## TPL-14 — Consultancy agreement

R1 · Premium · Guided

### Parties and engagement record

**[Client legal name, entity type, registration reference if applicable, address]** (Client) engages **[Consultant legal name, entity type, registration reference if applicable, address]** (Consultant). Agreement reference [ ]; date [ ]; start [ ]; completion/end [ ]. Client decision-maker [ ]; Consultant lead [ ].

This form concerns defined commercial analysis or advice. It does not authorise the Consultant to provide a regulated professional service, decide a person's employment/credit/medical outcome, make a government filing or bind the Client. Resolve any regulated-service element, actual party capacity, Nigeria/State/FCT law, data role and material schedule before signing. A business label does not waive mandatory protections.

### 1. Questions, methods and deliverables

The Consultant will address the questions, scope and deliverables in Schedule A with reasonable care and skill. The Consultant will distinguish the Client's supplied facts, independently checked facts, assumptions, estimates and recommendations. Material limitations, unavailable evidence and conflicts between sources will be disclosed in the deliverable. A scenario or forecast is not a promise of revenue, savings, funding, regulatory acceptance or another outcome.

The Client will provide the listed inputs and identify known limitations. The Consultant will seek clarification where information appears materially incomplete or inconsistent rather than knowingly present it as established. The Client remains responsible for its business decisions and implementation; this does not excuse the Consultant from its agreed standard of performance.

### 2. Review and changes

The Consultant will submit the agreed deliverables in the required format by the agreed milestones. The Client will provide a written acceptance or a specific explanation of non-conformity within the review period in Schedule A. The Consultant will correct non-conforming work within the agreed correction period without an additional charge. Silence or use for evaluation is not deemed acceptance. A disagreement about a recommendation is not itself non-conformity if the agreed work and evidential standard were met; an unsupported assertion or omitted agreed analysis remains reviewable.

A change in questions, evidence, scope or timing must be recorded with its fee and delivery effect and approved by both named decision-makers before extra work begins. No additional fee follows automatically from a meeting request or newly discovered uncertainty. If an input delay affects a milestone, the parties will agree the revised plan rather than assume the old date remains achievable.

### 3. Fees and expenses

Fees, currency, milestones, invoice timing and payment dates are stated in Schedule B. Time-based work requires the agreed time record; fixed-fee work is charged against its described milestone. Only expenses approved in advance within the recorded limit are recoverable. The Client will identify disputed invoice items and pay the undisputed amount on time. No default interest, tax rate or penalty is added by this form. Tax/withholding treatment must be recorded from the transaction's verified facts; a required deduction will be accompanied by its basis and supporting evidence when available.

### 4. Independence, conflicts and personnel

The Consultant will disclose an actual or reasonably foreseeable conflict affecting this engagement and propose a response. The Client may decline a proposed response and end the affected work with payment reconciliation under clause 8. The Consultant will not accept an undisclosed payment or benefit from a person whose offer it is evaluating for the Client.

Named key personnel may be replaced only after discussion and written agreement on a suitable substitute. Material subcontracting and associated access require prior written approval. The Consultant remains responsible for its contractual work. Neither party is the other's agent; a separate written authority is necessary to make a commitment on the other's behalf. No employment classification or statutory exemption is established by these labels.

### 5. Reports, underlying material and permitted reliance

The Consultant retains its pre-existing methods and materials. On payment of the agreed fee for the covered deliverable, it grants the Client a non-exclusive, worldwide, continuing licence to copy, use and adapt the identified final deliverables, or designated handover work under clause 8, for the purpose and audience in Schedule A, including through the Client's advisers who are bound to protect confidential material. Adaptations must not be presented as the Consultant's unchanged conclusions. Public circulation, resale or reliance for another purpose requires a separately agreed scope; lawful reporting or disclosure is not prohibited.

The Consultant will identify third-party material and restrictions before use and obtain permissions for the rights it promises. Confidential information belonging to another client must not be incorporated. Any desired assignment or exclusive licence requires a specific written replacement clause identifying the works and rights; payment for advice alone is not described here as transferring all underlying intellectual property.

### 6. Confidentiality and data

Each party will use the other's non-public information only for this engagement and protect it with reasonable care, limiting disclosure to people with a need to know and corresponding confidentiality duties. Exclusions are information demonstrably already known lawfully, independently developed, lawfully received without restriction or public without breach. Required legal disclosures may be made to the necessary extent, with advance notice where permitted. Nothing prevents a lawful report to a competent authority.

These duties apply during the engagement and for the confidentiality period after its end in Schedule C; restricted retained copies remain protected while held.

No confidential information or personal data may be placed in an unapproved AI system or external service. The parties will agree permitted tools and any redaction before use. Personal-data processing on the Client's behalf must not begin until roles, lawful instructions, security, locations, approved subprocessors and appropriate separate written terms are settled. Promptly report a suspected confidentiality or security incident affecting this engagement with the known facts and response steps; this does not replace assessment of any separate notification duty.

### 7. Responsibility and disruption

Each party is responsible for its breach and the conduct of persons for whom it is contractually responsible. This form adds no automatic indemnity, remedy waiver or liability cap. Any proposed limitation must be separately specified and reviewed for the actual engagement; non-excludable rights remain intact. The Consultant's absence of an outcome guarantee does not remove its obligation to perform the agreed work properly.

A party materially affected by an event outside its reasonable control will promptly notify the other, explain its effect, mitigate disruption and discuss a revised plan. Either may end the affected unperformed work if the disruption continues beyond the period selected in Schedule C. Properly earned fees remain subject to reconciliation, not automatic forfeiture of an advance.

### 8. Ending and handover

Either party may end on the convenience notice in Schedule C. A material remediable breach requires a notice describing the breach and the agreed remedy period; failure to remedy permits termination. A party may pause affected work that cannot continue lawfully or safely, stating the reason and scope promptly.

On ending, the Consultant will deliver completed paid-for work and a clearly labelled account of incomplete analysis, outstanding inputs and limitations. The Client pays for conforming work actually performed and pre-approved non-cancellable costs, credited against payments already made. Unearned advances are refunded. The Consultant will not label an unfinished report final. Each party will return or securely dispose of the other's confidential material under the agreed arrangements, retaining only what it has a verified basis to retain and continuing to protect it.

The termination record will identify completed and incomplete work handed over, its allocated settlement amount and the applicable clause 5 permissions. Payment of that allocated amount satisfies the payment condition for those identified rights; unperformed future work is not charged. Incomplete analysis retains its stated limitations and must not be represented as final advice. The record will specify its permitted handover/evaluation use. Rights already obtained in paid-for work are not revoked merely because the engagement ends.

### 9. Notices, disputes and entire agreement

Notices go to the recorded email/postal addresses with a reliable receipt record. The named leads will attempt resolution and then escalate to the decision-makers after the period in Schedule C. The governing law is the applicable law of Nigeria and **[specified State/FCT and relevant state-law selection]**; the chosen courts are those of competent jurisdiction in **[location, subject to mandatory jurisdiction rules]**. Urgent relief and lawful regulatory complaints are not barred. This is not an arbitration agreement.

This agreement and signed schedules record the engagement. Amendments require written agreement by authorised representatives; an amended schedule must identify any clause it changes. No general purchase-order terms are incorporated merely by quoting a reference. Nothing excludes fraud or a right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## TPL-15 — Non-disclosure agreement

R1 · Premium · Guided

### Parties, purpose and periods

This mutual agreement is between **[Party A legal name, entity type and address]** and **[Party B legal name, entity type and address]**. Agreement reference [ ]; date [ ]. Each is a Discloser when supplying its information and a Recipient when receiving the other's information.

Permitted purpose: **[describe the proposed discussion, project or evaluation narrowly]**. Disclosure period: [start] to [end]. Protection period for each disclosure: [agreed duration and start event]. Named contacts and permitted recipient categories: [ ]. Nigerian State/FCT and relevant governing-law choice: [ ]. Courts of competent jurisdiction/location: [ ]. Complete these entries and the signature authority before signing. This is not a non-compete, employment restraint, data-processing agreement or permission to conceal misconduct.

### 1. Confidential information

Confidential Information means non-public information disclosed for the permitted purpose that is marked confidential or that a reasonable recipient would understand to be confidential from its nature and context, including related notes or copies. Examples relevant to this exchange: [ ]. An oral or visual disclosure should be identified as confidential at disclosure and described in a follow-up record where practicable; omission of that record does not transform obviously confidential information into public information.

The Recipient may show that information falls outside this definition because it was lawfully known without restriction before disclosure, became public without breach of this agreement, was independently developed without use of the disclosed information, or was lawfully received from another source without a duty of confidence. A mixed document is protected only to the extent it contains protected information; these exclusions do not authorise disclosure of a protected combination merely because individual elements are public.

### 2. Use and protection

The Recipient will use Confidential Information only for the permitted purpose, protect it with reasonable care and at least the care it uses for its comparable confidential material, and restrict access to the agreed recipient categories who need to know and are bound to protect it. The Recipient remains responsible for disclosures it authorises to its representatives under this agreement. It will not publish the information, use it to solicit the Discloser's customers outside the permitted purpose, or copy it beyond what the purpose reasonably needs.

The Recipient will not upload the information to an unapproved AI system, public repository or external service. Approved tools and storage locations, if any: [ ]. No password, payment credential, sensitive personal record or unnecessary personal data should be exchanged under this agreement. If the purpose requires personal-data processing, the parties must separately settle the lawful scope, roles and appropriate processing terms before that activity begins; confidentiality alone is not that arrangement.

### 3. Required disclosures and protected communications

Where disclosure is required by applicable law or a binding order, the Recipient may disclose the required portion to the appropriate recipient. Where legally permitted, it will promptly notify the Discloser and reasonably cooperate, at the Discloser's expense, with a lawful effort to protect the information. This clause does not require disobeying an order or delaying a legally required disclosure.

Nothing in this agreement prevents a lawful report of suspected unlawful conduct, communication with a competent regulator or law-enforcement authority, obtaining confidential legal advice, or exercising a right that cannot lawfully be restricted. No prior consent is required for such a protected communication. This agreement must not be used to threaten someone for making one.

### 4. No transfer and no obligation to transact

The Discloser retains its rights in its information. The Recipient receives only permission to use it for the stated purpose; no patent, copyright, trademark or other ownership transfer is implied. Neither party is obliged to disclose particular information, continue negotiations, enter a transaction or grant exclusivity. Each will decide whether further verification or a separate commercial agreement is needed before relying on information for a transaction.

This agreement does not make all disclosed information a warranted commercial specification. That limitation does not excuse fraud, knowing misrepresentation or breach of an express promise in a separate agreement. A recipient must not present an unverified estimate or proposal as an approved commitment of the Discloser.

### 5. Incident response and return

The Recipient will promptly tell the Discloser's contact if it becomes aware of unauthorised access, loss or disclosure affecting the information, describe known facts and take reasonable containment steps. It will cooperate in addressing the incident and preserve relevant facts without making unsupported claims about cause or completion.

On a written request or the end of the purpose, the Recipient will stop using the information and return or securely delete copies within [agreed period], confirming what it has done. It may retain a restricted archival copy where a verified legal or recordkeeping basis requires retention, and routine backups that cannot reasonably be isolated immediately, provided they are not restored for ordinary use and remain protected until removed through the normal cycle. The Recipient will identify the retained category, basis and access restriction without disclosing protected legal advice. This is not permission for indefinite retention merely for convenience.

### 6. Duration and ending

Either party may stop future disclosures by written notice. Information already received remains protected for its agreed protection period, and clause 3 continues to apply. An extension or different treatment for a specifically identified category must be agreed in writing; this form does not silently create a perpetual restriction on every fact or an unverified trade-secret classification. Ending discussions does not erase a previous breach.

Restricted archival and backup copies permitted under clause 5 remain subject to its access and protection restrictions while retained, notwithstanding expiry of the general protection period. The lawful-disclosure and protected-communication exceptions continue to apply.

### 7. Remedies and disputes

Each party may seek remedies available under the applicable law for a breach. Neither automatic damages, an automatic injunction, a penalty nor an exclusive remedy is created by this agreement. No liability cap or waiver of non-excludable rights is supplied. The parties will first ask their named contacts to resolve a dispute and escalate to [decision-makers] after [agreed period], without preventing urgent lawful relief or a protected communication under clause 3.

This agreement is governed by the applicable laws of Nigeria and the State/FCT specified above. Disputes may be brought before the selected courts only to the extent those courts have jurisdiction; this clause cannot create jurisdiction they lack.

### 8. Notices and amendments

Notice addresses/email: Party A [ ]; Party B [ ]. Notices must identify this agreement and have a reliable receipt record; address changes must be notified. This does not substitute for court-service rules. Changes and any permitted assignment of this agreement require written agreement by both parties. This agreement and its completed fields record the confidentiality arrangement for this purpose; it does not replace a later service agreement unless that agreement expressly says so.
