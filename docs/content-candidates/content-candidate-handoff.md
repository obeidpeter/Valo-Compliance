## Integration requirements

1. Preserve stable inventory IDs and map candidate IDs to the final content register without silently replacing an existing entry. Titles or source files alone do not count toward release targets.
2. Keep provenance, draft labels, source paths, review checklists, release labels and engineering notes as internal metadata. Do not dump those authoring notes into the customer page or export. Customers should receive the useful guide/form body, appropriate scope and actual required publication disclosure.
3. Treat all candidates as untrusted draft inputs. Independently check them for accidental legal assertions, unsupported factual claims, unsuitable data collection, incomplete fields and misleading certainty. Retain separate provider/run evidence for the factual and challenge reviews.
4. Retain actual rights evidence for this authoring route and any subsequent provider output. Original provenance does not by itself establish a licence or commercial acceptance. None of these files asserts specialist review.
5. For forms, transform the approved content into canonical editable structures and valid DOCX/PDF layouts. The wide Markdown tables are content specifications, not a claim of print-ready design. Render, inspect and refine appropriate page orientation, column groups and page breaks.
6. Preserve empty/unknown states and decimal-safe arithmetic. Fictional guide examples never populate a customer's document. A business manager's internal review or payment record is not a Valo publication decision or fabricated evidence of an event.
7. Release through the actual immutable-manifest publisher only after applicable gates pass. Premium sales remain subject to the separately required commercial decisions. Held items remain absent from public release counts.
8. Link published guides and industry pages to real eligible items and accurate coverage. Do not generate dead links or expose held content to fill an empty page.
