# Development publication of the reminder and inventory guides

On 22 November 2026 the owner authorized publishing version 1 of GUIDE-C03, “Follow up an unpaid invoice with a useful reminder”, and version 1 of GUIDE-C04, “Keep an inventory register people can maintain”, in development if they pass their reviews.

For each article, the factual reviewer and the independent challenge reviewer must both pass the same prepared candidate. Keep its original preparation policy and verify its retained text, worked example, rights and Word and PDF evidence. The dedicated publisher checks each release. The usual withdrawal and expiry controls continue to apply when either guide is read.
