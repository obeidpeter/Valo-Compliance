## Working changes

- Native Zod **4.6.7** replaces the old 3.25.76 package, including editorial
  `/v4` consumers. Orval generates native-v4 validators. The installed API,
  database, core and provider peer graphs resolve the patched package.

  An excessive constrained-array test rejects 10,000 entries without invoking
  the item validator.

  used the actual old runtime: ten canonical fixtures and two profile fixtures
  retained their hashes. Two previously saved revisions and one new revision
  produced **six real private DOCX/PDF exports**, subsequently read through the
  authorised storage path. Only that proof's synthetic workspace and six
  private objects were removed; provider usage history was untouched.
- The twelve exact free operational source candidates now import from the
  hash-bound intake manifest. Customer subsection headings are retained;
  field specifications, filled examples, engineering metadata and neighbouring
  forms are excluded. This is draft intake, **not twelve published templates
  or twelve new customer editors**.
- New TPL-07 preparation uses the corrected inventory source, with its exact
  file/manifest lineage and applicable official ChatGPT output-permission text.
  It does not inherit an earlier candidate's reviews or pretend that an
  upstream server-authoring receipt exists. Provider permission is not
  copyright certification, third-party clearance or commercial licence approval.
- Export eligibility now executes real ZIP/XML, standard-reader PDF/A4,
  geometry, complete-content and full-disclosure checks. The publisher reads
  the actual private bytes and repeats these checks; generic pass lists,
  altered metadata, corrupt formats and mismatching attestations fail.
