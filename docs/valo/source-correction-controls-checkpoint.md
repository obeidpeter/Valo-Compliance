# R1 source corrections and staff safety controls

This change implements existing R1 requirements ADM-001/003/004/007/009, EDP-011, CMP-005/007, RUL-009 and DOC-007. It does not unlock R2, approve a source, accept an evaluation corpus or authorize spending.

Registered, allowlisted official sources receive durable daily jobs. The existing bounded DNS/redirect/body/extraction/private-storage path captures immutable source snapshots. Jobs have bounded attempts and leases; a stale owner cannot finish a reclaimed job. A capture timeout records uncertainty and prevents another capture in that process until the original operation settles. Retrieval alone never renews a substantive review. General-risk material has a quarterly review boundary; other risks have a monthly boundary, in addition to existing source effective dates and explicit review expiry.

Release bindings preserve each distinct bundle source ID, its exact passage hash and its retained full-source snapshot. Multiple passages from the same official URL share a monitor without collapsing their identities. New publication still requires every existing rights, review, mechanical and export gate. The bounded publisher verifies passages against retained private extracted bytes and binds the current capture; it cannot clear an old version's safety hold.

A changed/unavailable/overdue source immediately makes its bound versions ineligible. Dependency and derived-rule fan-out use durable cursors and at most 50 targets per transaction; customer fan-out handles at most 200 records per phase. Exact-version impacts retain actor, reason and old/new snapshot IDs. They create a review-work record without running or impersonating drafting, factual or challenge reviews. Source-linked tasks are marked for review and unsent reminders are cancelled. Historical assessment facts/results/hashes and customer documents/files are preserved. Private notices are scoped to affected workspaces and do not disclose internal staff reasons. Privacy quarantine prevents new notices; verified erasure includes their separate cleanup phase.

Content staff can register an existing official source and hold/withdraw an exact content or rule version. System staff can independently pause AI or new checkout. Each write rechecks its own active area role and freshly verified MFA after acquiring the safety lock, requires a reason, and binds an idempotency key to actor and payload. Sensitive action audits are append-only. Resume removes only that safety pause; financial, merchant, source, publication and development gates remain in force. There is no publish, review-pass, role-grant or arbitrary-URL command.

Final source-dependent task/reminder, master-download and new checkout actions share the source-state lock. Managed inference checks the AI safety pause before reservation and keeps the read lock through the admitted provider operation. A pause/hold committed first prevents later dispatch; an already-started external operation cannot be recalled. Unaffected released guidance remains readable. Workspace mutation transactions now reuse the canonical membership/tombstone authorization after their workspace lock.

The staff UI exposes current version safety, monitored sources, queued correction work and service pauses. Workspace correction notices are a separate authenticated, paginated view. OpenAPI and generated clients describe the new routes.

Local verification: two focused boundary tests pass, covering source identity/freshness/holds, multiple passages at one URL and rejection of forged staff/source/publication fields. The native PostgreSQL test is registered for the disposable CI database, with two affected tenants and an unaffected tenant, 202 affected tasks, retained assessment hashes, actual child-process death and lease recovery, HTTP origin/role/MFA checks, queued staff/customer revocation, privacy tombstones and final-dispatch ordering. Native execution and full CI results must be reported from the exact committed head; they are not implied by this document.

There are no newly eligible publications, real provider-review receipts, paid calls or external messages from this change. Source-based rule publishing and the remaining source-content preparation/evaluation adapters still need their separately required implementation/evidence. The in-app review queue is not proof that an external owner alert was delivered. Production scheduling, staffing and external delivery remain distinct operating evidence. Migration 0029 is additive and must be integrated with the development migration journal in timestamp order; it is not automatically applied to the native Replit database.
