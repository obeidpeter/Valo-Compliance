## What is Replit AI Integrations?

When you use Replit AI Integrations, Replit manages the provider credentials and
bills you at the public API price. Your usage is billed to your Replit credits
and appears on your usage page, broken down by Existing Replit App.

## Billing

You're billed at the public API price set by the provider. Costs are deducted
from your Replit credits and appear on your usage page.
