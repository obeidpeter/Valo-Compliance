### 10. INTERNAL AI EDITORIAL SYSTEM — CRITICAL

Implement requirements **EDP-001 through EDP-012** in their entirety.

This system must be real, observable, versioned and capable of operating after launch.

It must not be a simple prompt that asks an AI model whether content is correct.

Implement distinct internal capabilities:

**A. Official source retrieval**

Retrieve and preserve authoritative legislative and government-source material.

Capture issuing authority, document title, passage, jurisdiction, effective dates, retrieval timestamp, version and source hash.

**B. Evidence-grounded drafting**

Generate template or guidance candidates only from the permitted content scope and supporting evidence.

Maintain claim-to-source mappings.

**C. Independent AI factual review**

Check whether the candidate accurately represents the retrieved sources.

**D. Independent AI challenge**

Check for omitted exceptions, incorrect jurisdictions, misleading certainty, outdated provisions, contradictory information and unsupported assumptions.

The challenge process must be separate from the drafting process.

**E. Enhanced risk validation**

Apply more stringent checks to tax, employment, contracts, governance, privacy and regulated-sector content.

**F. Deterministic validation**

Validate structured schemas, dates, numeric values, required fields, rights evidence, applicability boundaries and test fixtures in application code.

**G. Immutable publication manifests**

Bind the content version, source evidence, review results, model configurations, prompt versions, policies and test outcomes to a publication manifest.

**H. Automated publication**

The independently permissioned publisher may publish eligible content automatically when all mandatory gates pass.

Do not require routine per-item human or professional sign-off.

However, no AI confidence score or model agreement may override a failed evidence or deterministic validation gate.

**I. Holding and withdrawal**

Content with missing evidence, unresolved conflicts, failed tests or unsupported material claims must remain held.

The system must support rapid suspension of erroneous content.

**J. Source-change monitoring**

Monitor configured authoritative sources for changes.

Run reassessment and publication workflows when substantive changes are identified.

**K. User-impact management**

When relevant published content changes, identify affected recommendations, tasks and reminders.

Do not silently rewrite user-created documents or historical assessments.

**L. Auditability**

Retain detailed records of draft creation, review findings, source references, release decisions, corrections and withdrawals.

Use the exact EDP requirements and acceptance standards from the TRD, including the required editorial evaluation suite.

The customer-facing drafting AI and internal editorial AI must have separately scoped permissions and operational records.
