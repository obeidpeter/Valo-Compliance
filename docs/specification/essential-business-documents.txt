COMPLETE LIST OF BUSINESS & NGO REGISTRATIONS IN NIGERIA

For a business or non-profit organisation operating in Nigeria, registrations fall into several categories: legal establishment, taxation, employment, regulatory compliance, industry-specific licensing, intellectual property, and non-profit governance.

Not every registration is compulsory for every organisation. The requirements depend on the organisation’s structure, location, activities, workforce and industry.

A. CAC & LEGAL ENTITY REGISTRATIONS

* Business Name Registration
* Private Limited Liability Company Registration (Ltd)
* Public Limited Liability Company Registration (Plc)
* Company Limited by Guarantee Registration (Ltd/Gte)
* Unlimited Liability Company Registration
* Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Registration
* Limited Partnership (LP) Registration
* Incorporated Trustees Registration (NGOs, foundations, associations, religious bodies)
* Cooperative Society Registration (through the relevant cooperative registrar)

These establish the organisation’s legal structure. The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) administers the first eight categories. Corporate Affairs Commission +2

B. TAX & REVENUE REGISTRATIONS

* Tax Identification (Tax ID) Registration or Retrieval
* Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) Corporate Taxpayer Registration
* Value Added Tax (VAT) Registration
* State Internal Revenue Service Taxpayer Registration
* Pay-As-You-Earn (PAYE) Employer Registration
* NRS E-Invoicing / Merchant Buyer Solution (MBS) Onboarding, where applicable

2026 update: The Nigeria Revenue Service has replaced the former FIRS framework, and the new Tax ID replaces the previous TIN identifier. The e-invoicing framework also has its own onboarding requirements. Tax ID +3

C. EMPLOYER & EMPLOYEE REGISTRATIONS

* Nigeria Social Insurance Trust Fund (NSITF) Employer Registration
* National Pension Commission (PenCom) Employer Code Registration
* Employee Retirement Savings Account (RSA) Registration
* Industrial Training Fund (ITF) Employer Registration
* National Health Insurance / Applicable State Health Insurance Scheme Registration

These support pension, employee compensation, workforce training and health insurance obligations. The applicable requirements vary by employer and scheme. NSITF +3

D. GENERAL BUSINESS & REGULATORY REGISTRATIONS

* Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML) Registration, for covered businesses and at-risk NGOs
* Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) Registration, for qualifying data controllers and processors
* Small and Medium Enterprises Development Agency of Nigeria (SMEDAN) Registration, voluntary
* Bureau of Public Procurement (BPP) Contractor/Consultant Registration, where applicable
* State Business Premises Registration
* Local Government Trade or Business Operating Permit, where applicable
* Signage and Outdoor Advertising Permit
* Fire Safety Inspection and Certification
* Environmental Permits, including NESREA permits where applicable
* Building Development or Change-of-Use Approval
* Environmental Health and Sanitation Permits, where required
* Waste Management Registration or Permit, where applicable

SCUML, NDPC and environmental requirements are particularly activity-dependent. scuml.efcc.gov.ng +3

E. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY REGISTRATIONS

* Trademark Registration
* Patent Registration
* Industrial Design Registration
* Copyright Voluntary Registration / Notification

These help organisations document or protect their brands, inventions, designs and creative works. Copyright protection generally arises automatically; registration is not a prerequisite. Home +1

F. INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC REGISTRATIONS & LICENCES

* NAFDAC Product Registration, for regulated food, cosmetics, medicines, medical devices and other products
* Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON) MANCAP Certification
* SONCAP Product Conformity Certification, for applicable imports
* Nigerian Export Promotion Council (NEPC) Exporter Registration
* Customs Importer Registration / Trade Portal Onboarding, where applicable
* Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission (NIPC) Registration, for foreign-participation investments
* Federal Ministry of Interior Business Permit, for qualifying foreign-participation companies
* Expatriate Quota Approval and Relevant Immigration Registration
* Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) Banking or Financial Institution Licensing
* CBN Payment Service Provider Licensing
* Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Capital Market Operator Registration
* Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC) Digital Money Lender Approval
* Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) Telecommunications Licensing
* Electricity Generation, Distribution or Trading Licences, through NERC or the relevant state regulator
* Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) Licences and Service Permits
* Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority (NMDPRA) Licences and Permits
* Healthcare Facility Registration and Licensing
* Pharmacy / Pharmaceutical Premises Registration
* Educational Institution Registration and Accreditation
* Hospitality and Tourism Business Licensing
* Real Estate Practitioner or Agency Registration, where required
* Engineering and Construction Professional Registration, where applicable
* Aviation Business Licensing
* Maritime Operator Registration and Licensing
* Mining and Mineral Title Registration
* Private Security Company Licensing
* Technology Transfer Agreement Registration (NOTAP), where applicable
* Financial Reporting Council (FRC) Audit and Assurance Firm Registration

The applicable licences differ considerably between industries. For example, NAFDAC regulates specified product categories, while the CBN, SEC and NCC oversee distinct financial and communications activities. NAFDAC +8

G. ADDITIONAL NON-PROFIT ORGANISATION REGISTRATIONS

For NGOs, charities, foundations, faith-based organisations and associations, the following may apply in addition to the earlier registrations.

* SCUML Registration for At-Risk Non-Profit Organisations, where applicable
* State-Level NGO Registration, where required
* International NGO Cooperation or Government Authorisation, where applicable
* Relevant Programme or Facility Licensing, such as schools, clinics or residential care facilities
* Tax Exemption Documentation or Recognition, where applicable

An NGO’s CAC incorporation does not automatically exempt it from every tax obligation or operational licence. SCUML also applies according to the relevant risk-based requirements. scuml.efcc.gov.ng +2

H. POST-REGISTRATION FILINGS & ONGOING COMPLIANCE

These are not new forms of business incorporation, but they are important to maintaining a registered organisation:

* CAC Annual Returns Filing
* Persons with Significant Control (PSC) Disclosure
* Directors’ and Company Secretaries’ Changes
* Registered Office Address Changes
* Share Capital Changes and Share Allotment Filings
* Registration of Company Charges and Debentures
* Corporate Name Changes and Re-registration
* Tax Returns and Statutory Remittances
* Pension, Employee Compensation and Other Statutory Returns
* Regulatory Licence Renewals and Updates

The CAC maintains specific procedures for many of these post-incorporation filings. Corporate Affairs Commission - CAC

Important distinction

This is a broad national checklist of 79 registration, licensing and compliance items, not 79 certificates that every business must obtain. Some are alternative legal structures, some are optional, some are regulatory permissions, and others are ongoing filings. Further state-specific and industry-specific requirements may apply.

For practical business management, I would organise the documents and registrations into four separate files:

* Business Formation File: CAC documents, legal structure and ownership records.
* Tax & Statutory Compliance File: Tax ID, VAT, PAYE, pensions, employee compensation and e-invoicing.
* Operating Licences File: Sector approvals, premises permits and other regulatory permissions.
* Compliance & Renewal File: Annual returns, regulatory filings, certificates and renewal deadlines.

That distinction matters because registering a business, legally operating a business and maintaining compliance are three different responsibilities.
