01 / READ FIRST
Purpose, status and build baseline
Valo will give Nigerian SME owners useful business documents and source-linked compliance guidance. This revision adopts AI drafting, AI review and automated content publication because Nigerian legal, tax and sector specialists are not currently available. No per-item professional or human content sign-off is required. The product owner remains accountable for the operating policy and platform release; the controls below define what the AI pipeline may publish.
Confirmed product decisions
DECISION | BASELINE
Audience | All Nigerian SMEs, across industries; microbusinesses and proposed businesses can also explore the platform.
Core offer | Template preview/download, guided document customisation and genuine AI-assisted drafting; compliance guidance and service discovery.
Business model | Free compliance guidance; selected free templates and paid premium templates. Future professional bookings remain part of the product direction.
Experience | Minimalist, premium and professional; mobile-friendly, plain English and Nigerian business examples.
Build environment | Replit is the intended development environment. Production infrastructure must pass the foundation-stage technical and privacy review.

Proposed decisions, not previously approved
This baseline proposes a single-business workspace at launch, one-off template purchases and bundles, a managed identity service, a modular application architecture and a 20-week planning envelope. Team invitations, subscriptions and live professional bookings are later releases. Content quantities, supported drafting types and service targets are planning recommendations.
What “full build” means here
The first commercial release must have real accounts, server-authorised access, durable storage, real payment verification, usable DOCX/PDF exports, an AI-reviewed content system, operational support and tested recovery. A simulated checkout, localStorage-only account, fake AI response or fictional provider is not production functionality.
NOT AN AUDIT  No existing Valo codebase, database or deployment was inspected for this document. Reuse of any earlier mock-up is conditional on an engineering and security assessment; there is no assumption that it is production-ready.

Interpretation: “Must” is release-blocking for the stated release. “Should” may move only with a recorded owner decision. R1 = commercial core; R2 = growth and service coordination; R3 = selective marketplace expansion. The companion Product Roadmap uses the same release names and requirement IDs.

02 / PRODUCT CONTRACT
Scope and release boundaries
RELEASE | INCLUDED | EXPLICIT LIMIT
R1 / commercial core | Public site; templates; 12 guided workflows; 4 AI drafting types; free compliance hub and checker; owner workspace; payments; email reminders; service enquiries; admin CMS. | No automatic government filing, legal sign-off or universal state/local coverage.
R2 / growth | Team access; more AI-reviewed templates and locations; scoped AI clause assistance; bundles/optional subscriptions; provider onboarding; quote and appointment requests. | Do not collect provider payments until contracts, refunds, settlement and support are operational.
R3 / selective expansion | Controlled paid-booking pilot; additional regulated sectors; integrations and optional specialist review; public API only if justified. | No escrow, client-money custody, lending, tax filing or e-signature service without a separately approved specification.

R1 content and capability floor
Target 40 AI-reviewed templates: 12 free and 28 premium, of which 12 have guided editors. Provide four customer AI drafting workflows: invoice, quotation, payment reminder and business proposal. Target 30 compliance guidance entries, 12 practical guides, 20 industry categories and six deeper packs. Only items passing EDP-001–012 count; a shortfall requires a recorded scope or schedule change, never bypassed checks.
All 36 states and the FCT are selectable. Prioritise Lagos, FCT and Bayelsa for initial state-level source checks. Businesses elsewhere receive relevant AI-reviewed federal guidance and explicit local coverage gaps. No LGA is described as fully covered without an evidence-backed inventory. AI review is not professional verification or certification.
Commercial and operational defaults
Users may browse guidance, previews and checker results without paying. Saving a private workspace or draft requires a verified account. Premium entitlements attach to the purchasing business workspace. Actual template prices, tax treatment, licence wording and refund policy must be approved before live sales; do not carry sample prices into production.
Planning capacity, not a demand forecast
Use 5,000 registered businesses, 100 concurrent active sessions and a 30-minute load test at 20 ordinary API requests per second as the initial engineering test envelope. Test five concurrent export jobs separately. These are sizing assumptions to validate, not claims about existing customers or a promise of unlimited scale.
COVERAGE IS NOT CERTIFICATION  Selecting an industry or completing tasks must never produce a “fully compliant” badge. Valo reports AI-reviewed guidance, user actions and remaining uncertainty.


03 / ACCESS MODEL
Users, permissions and core journeys
ROLE | ALLOWED | RESTRICTIONS
Visitor | Read guidance, search, preview templates, use anonymous checker. | No private workspace access or premium export.
Business owner | Manage own business profile, buy templates, edit/export drafts, manage tasks and enquiries. | One business per workspace in R1; no access to another tenant.
Team editor / viewer | R2: edit or view specifically authorised business content. | No billing, ownership transfer or invitations unless separately granted.
AI drafting and review services | Draft templates, guidance and rules; run separate evidence and risk checks. | Separate run identities; no direct publish, policy changes or customer-data access.
AI publisher / content owner | Publisher releases eligible versions; owner monitors queues and can pause/withdraw. | Hash-bound gates; no per-item human sign-off or general access to private drafts.
Support / finance | Support sees ticket metadata; finance sees orders, reconciliation and refunds. | Document access requires scoped, time-limited customer permission and an audit entry.
Platform administrator | Manage configuration, incidents, role grants and controlled recovery. | MFA, least privilege and audited break-glass access; no routine unrestricted browsing.

End-to-end journeys that must work
J1 / Document: search → understand the licence → preview → buy when required → customise → save → export → reopen on another device.
J2 / Requirements: describe a business → see reasons and gaps → open a sourced requirement → create a personal task → follow the official service link.
J3 / AI draft: choose a supported type → supply missing details → review generated sections → correct → save a revision → export.
J4 / Service help: read a requirement → choose a service category → explicitly consent to share an enquiry → receive a real acknowledgement → track its status.
J5 / Content operations: retrieve sources → AI draft → separate AI checks → deterministic tests → automated publish or hold → notify affected users when material → retain prior versions.
J6 / Recovery: interrupted checkout or export → safe retry → one entitlement or file job → clear status, without duplicate charges or silent draft loss.
Permission checks must run on the server for every route, API operation, job and file retrieval. Hiding a navigation item is not access control. Anonymous checker responses use temporary, non-sensitive inputs and are not retained by default.

04 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Public experience, identity and profile
WEB-001  Equal product prominence  [R1 · Must] The homepage and primary navigation must give documents and compliance guidance comparable visibility. Accept: both journeys are reachable within one deliberate action from the hero and mobile menu.
WEB-002  Discovery and accessibility  [R1 · Must] Provide library, compliance, industries, resources, services, pricing, support and legal pages; search/filter URLs must be shareable. Accept: keyboard, empty, loading, error and mobile states are covered for every primary route.
WEB-003  Public search  [R1 · Must] Search approved public templates, guidance, industries, articles and service categories, with type and industry filters. Accept: unpublished content and customer drafts never appear; a curated 50-query relevance test meets its agreed pass threshold.
WEB-004  Discoverability and honesty  [R1 · Must] Render public content with meaningful metadata, canonical URLs and a sitemap; exclude accounts, drafts and admin pages. Accept: no fictional testimonials, customers, ratings or regulator endorsements are displayed in production.
IAM-001  Real customer identity  [R1 · Must] Use verified email-based sign-in and recovery, with optional approved social login. A Replit account must not be required. Accept: verification, expired-link, logout, recovery and suspended-account tests pass.
IAM-002  Secure sessions and privileged access  [R1 · Must] Use the managed identity provider’s supported session flow, secure cookies and server validation. Require MFA for staff and administrators. Accept: revoked sessions cannot call private endpoints; staff role changes are audited.
IAM-003  Tenant boundaries  [R1 · Must] Resolve membership server-side and scope all private queries, jobs and object reads to the authorised workspace. Accept: changing a workspace, draft, order or file identifier cannot reveal another business’s data.
PRO-001  Business profile  [R1 · Must] Capture legal form, registration status, sector/activity, state/FCT, optional LGA, employees, turnover band and year, premises, manufacturing/importing and data-processing characteristics. Accept: “unknown” is valid and leads to uncertainty, not automatic exemption.
PRO-002  Privacy and lifecycle  [R1 · Must] Separate necessary processing notices from optional marketing consent; support profile correction, account export and deletion requests. Accept: the user can decline marketing and still use the product; consent versions and changes are recorded.
PRO-003  Reassessment  [R1 · Must] Version relevant profile changes and offer a new requirements assessment without overwriting the prior result. Accept: changed location, activities or employee count produces a dated comparison and preserves user-created tasks.

05 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Template library and licensing
DOC-001  Structured catalogue  [R1 · Must] Each template must have an ID, slug, category, use case, industry tags, risk class, version, AI-review status, format, rights record and price/entitlement reference. Accept: incomplete evidence or failed publication gates prevent release; no professional reviewer identity is fabricated.
DOC-002  Useful discovery  [R1 · Must] Provide keyword search, category/industry/free-premium filters and relevance/name/recently-checked sorting; persist filters in the URL. Accept: combinations, pagination, reset and no-results suggestions work without exposing paid source files.
DOC-003  Decision-ready detail  [R1 · Must] Explain purpose, use, required inputs, limitations, formats, related guidance and revision date. Show AI drafting/review status and absence of professional review. Accept: higher-risk templates explain when independent advice may be useful and never promise enforceability, suitability or official approval.
DOC-004  Safe previews  [R1 · Must] Serve a separately generated public preview that identifies sample fields and access conditions. Accept: restricted template content and downloadable masters are absent from public bundles, page source and unauthorised storage responses.
DOC-005  Owned downloads  [R1 · Must] Free templates may be downloaded after the chosen account gate; paid downloads require a valid server-checked entitlement. Accept: successful exports appear in the business library and remain linked to the template version used.
DOC-006  Content ownership and licence  [R1 · Must] Retain creation provenance, provider terms and commercial-use permissions for original, commissioned or licensed material. AI output alone does not prove rights clearance. Proposed licence: one business’s internal use, no template resale. Accept: source-checked terms pass editorial gates and the product owner accepts commercial terms before sales; counsel sign-off is not required by this plan.
DOC-007  Version and withdrawal handling  [R1 · Must] Keep purchased historical versions and generated drafts traceable. Warn about material updates; withdraw unsafe masters from new use and explain available remediation. Accept: an update never silently rewrites a customer’s saved document.
DOC-008  Government forms are separate  [R1 · Must] Identify official forms by authority and official URL; do not rebrand preparation checklists as official filing forms. Accept: any downloadable government form has checked reuse rights, provenance and a current-link warning.
DOC-009  Saved items and coverage  [R1 · Must] Allow signed-in owners to save/unsave templates and see relevant recommendations. Unsupported formats or templates must be labelled unavailable rather than simulated. Accept: favourites persist across devices and deleted records degrade gracefully.
MONETISATION BOUNDARY  Guidance, applicability explanations and official-source links remain free. Buying a template must not be presented as a legal condition for satisfying a government requirement.


06 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Guided editor, revision and export
EDT-001  Guided forms  [R1 · Must] Implement the 12 designated guided templates using typed field schemas, conditional questions, examples and server validation. Accept: required placeholders cannot be omitted silently, and inappropriate clauses are not selected solely from a broad industry label.
EDT-002  Canonical document model  [R1 · Must] Persist a structured document with paragraphs, lists, tables, field values, selected clauses, formatting tokens and template version. Accept: preview, DOCX and PDF use the same saved revision, not three independently generated texts.
EDT-003  Review and editing  [R1 · Must] Support section-level text editing, undo, preview and a clear draft status. Accept: changes remain after reload and login on another device; locked guidance is distinguished from editable business content.
EDT-004  Autosave and concurrency  [R1 · Must] Debounce saves, show saving/saved/offline states and send an expected revision number. Reject stale writes with a conflict response. Accept: two open tabs cannot silently overwrite each other; retry preserves unsaved text in memory.
EDT-005  Missing-data protection  [R1 · Must] Highlight unresolved fields, inconsistent dates and missing party details. Accept: final export blocks mandatory omissions or requires a deliberately watermarked incomplete draft; never invent legal names, addresses or signatures.
EDT-006  Real file generation  [R1 · Must] Produce editable DOCX and readable A4 PDF outputs through a server-side render job. Accept: each guided template opens in Word and a standard PDF reader; long names, multi-page tables and the naira symbol render correctly. [S22–S23]
EDT-007  Durable export jobs  [R1 · Must] Create an idempotent job keyed to document revision, format and render version; provide progress, retry and failure detail. Accept: worker restart does not lose the job and repeated requests do not create uncontrolled duplicate exports.
EDT-008  Private retrieval  [R1 · Must] Stream authorised files through the application or use short-lived scoped URLs only where supported and tested. Accept: expired/revoked access fails; browsers and shared caches do not retain private responses as public assets.
EDT-009  Bounded formatting  [R1 · Must] Support a tested set of typography, lists, tables, headers and branding options. R1 excludes arbitrary DOCX import, real-time co-editing, embedded macros and electronic signature execution. Accept: unsupported content cannot trigger remote network fetches or code execution.
EDT-010  Ownership after changes  [R1 · Must] Saved drafts remain the customer’s work even when an entitlement is refunded or a master is retired; future reuse/export policy must be disclosed. Accept: revoking platform access is never described as retracting a previously downloaded local file.

07 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
AI-assisted document drafting
The four customer drafting workflows are separate from the internal AI editorial pipeline in EDP-001–012. That pipeline may draft, review and publish templates, guidance and rules within the configured scope. The customer checker still executes released rules deterministically. AI output is not a professional opinion or compliance certification. Review provider data settings per endpoint. [S18]
AI-001  Supported tasks and user control  [R1 · Must] Allow invoice, quotation, payment-reminder and business-proposal drafting. Ask targeted follow-up questions and show a structured draft before acceptance. Accept: unsupported customer legal requests are directed to a scoped published resource, official authority or independent adviser; no adviser availability is implied.
AI-002  Bounded generation  [R1 · Must] Provide the chosen template schema and permitted fields/sections; validate the returned structured result before rendering. Accept: invalid output is retried once under policy, then fails safely with the manual editor available.
AI-003  No invented business facts  [R1 · Must] Preserve supplied names and amounts; mark missing facts explicitly. Calculate totals deterministically in application code using decimal-safe arithmetic. Accept: the model cannot invent a tax rate, registration number, approval, signature or payment receipt.
AI-004  Governed applicability  [R1 · Must] Keep customer assessments deterministic and source-governed. Customer AI may explain released text but cannot invent obligations, deadlines or exemptions. Internal AI may propose and publish changes only through EDP-001–012. Accept: ungrounded claims and unreleased rules never enter live assessments.
AI-005  Privacy-aware input  [R1 · Must] Explain what is sent to the model; minimise fields and redact unnecessary identifiers. Never send another tenant’s data or secrets. Accept: test payloads prove only the selected document context is transmitted and no customer content enters generic analytics.
AI-006  Injection and tool controls  [R1 · Must] Treat user text and retrieved content as untrusted data. Customer drafting calls have no filing, email, payment or arbitrary browsing tools. Internal source retrieval and publication use separately scoped services under EDP-009. Accept: prompts cannot override permissions, change policy or execute unauthorised actions.
AI-007  Traceability and quotas  [R1 · Must] Record model/configuration, prompt-template version, latency, outcome and token/cost metadata without retaining raw prompts in routine logs. Enforce workspace and global limits. Accept: a quota breach stops new AI calls without blocking manual document access.
AI-008  Customer drafting quality gate  [R1 · Must] Maintain 60 source/rubric-backed cases across the four tasks, missing inputs and adversarial text. Accept: at least 95% meet the rubric, every financial calculation is correct and no critical leak or invented legal claim remains. Keep this separate from the editorial suite in EDP-010; neither measures certified legal accuracy.
AI-009  Graceful failure  [R1 · Must] Use timeouts, bounded retries and a feature kill switch; return useful guidance during provider failure. Accept: a model outage does not break paid downloads, free guidance or the deterministic editor.

08 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Compliance guidance and provenance
Nigeria’s current tax transition requires effective-dated content and a distinction between historical periods and current obligations. The official 2026 transition announcement supports that design; this specification deliberately does not hard-code disputed or unreviewed tax thresholds. [S01–S02]
CMP-001  Required content structure  [R1 · Must] Record authority, jurisdiction, activity/legal form, conditions, action, evidence, source, effective period, AI-review status and related templates. Accept: missing authoritative evidence, scope or review manifest blocks publication of the affected claim; a human reviewer is not a required field.
CMP-002  Applicability language  [R1 · Must] Use “Likely relevant”, “May apply”, “More information needed” or “Not indicated by these answers”. Accept: no output states that the business is legally exempt or fully compliant merely because a questionnaire did not trigger a rule.
CMP-003  Federal, state and local separation  [R1 · Must] Represent federal, state/FCT and LGA requirements separately, including activity-specific overlaps. Accept: a Lagos-only entry cannot appear as a national requirement, and an unknown LGA never receives invented local obligations.
CMP-004  Sources and review dates  [R1 · Must] Store official URL, title, passage locator, retrieved text/snapshot hash, effective dates and supported interpretation. Separate sourceFetchedAt, aiReviewedAt and publishedAt. Accept: labels say “AI reviewed” or “Source checked”; “professionally reviewed” appears only if that review actually occurred and was recorded.
CMP-005  Legal updates and historic periods  [R1 · Must] Version changes; preserve superseded interpretations and period applicability; show correction notices when material. Accept: running the same profile under two rule versions remains reproducible and explains changed recommendations.
CMP-006  Deadlines and fees  [R1 · Must] Show a deadline, rate, threshold or fee only when its scope and source are approved. Distinguish government fees from Valo/provider charges. Accept: missing evidence shows “Confirm with the authority”; an assumed due date is never scheduled automatically.
CMP-007  Freshness and conflicts  [R1 · Must] Monitor source changes, failed retrieval and review expiry. Trigger an AI reassessment; hold affected high-impact claims until evidence and tests pass. Accept: stale or unresolved numeric rules cannot generate definite recommendations or new statutory reminders; show an uncertainty notice and official route.
CMP-008  AI editorial control  [R1 · Must] Apply EDP-001–012 to tax, employment, privacy and sector material, with stricter evidence checks for high-impact content. Accept: separate drafting and review runs plus deterministic gates control automated publication. No Nigerian specialist or second human approval is required; a named owner can pause or withdraw content.
CMP-009  Free and understandable  [R1 · Must] Explain the practical next step, why it may matter and what information is missing in plain English. Accept: the source and basic guidance remain readable without purchase and are not disguised professional endorsements.
The source register distinguishes retrieved material from instruments only located in the original baseline. The pipeline must obtain the authoritative text and retain claim-level evidence before publication. A located title, inaccessible document or model recollection cannot clear a rule for release.

09 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Personalised requirements checker
RUL-001  Configurable questionnaire  [R1 · Must] Use progressive questions driven by activity, legal form, locations, employment, turnover period, product type and personal-data handling. Accept: irrelevant questions are skipped, progress is clear, and the user can return to previous answers.
RUL-002  Unknown is a first-class state  [R1 · Must] Represent unknown, unanswered and not-applicable separately from false/zero. Accept: missing turnover, workforce or location information generates a follow-up or uncertainty label, never an exemption.
RUL-003  Deterministic rules  [R1 · Must] Execute versioned declarative conditions using allowlisted operators. AI may author rules offline through EDP gates, but cannot create or modify them during a customer assessment. Accept: equal input, assessment date and released ruleset version give equal results; arbitrary code is rejected.
RUL-004  Explainability  [R1 · Must] Return matched facts, requirement ID/version, jurisdiction, source references, confidence label and missing facts. Accept: every recommendation exposes a plain-English “Why am I seeing this?” explanation.
RUL-005  Threshold-safe evaluation  [R1 · Must] Where an approved rule has a numeric threshold, compare the proper metric, currency, accounting period and effective date. Accept: a broad turnover band that straddles a threshold requests more information instead of forcing a result.
RUL-006  Complete but bounded results  [R1 · Must] Separate recommended documents, potentially relevant requirements, unanswered questions and next steps. Accept: coverage gaps and excluded high-risk sectors are visible; no compliance score implies a legal certification.
RUL-007  Versioned assessment records  [R1 · Must] Persist the consented profile snapshot, rule versions, evaluation date and result hashes for signed-in users. Accept: users can compare assessments; anonymous answers are cleared unless they explicitly choose to save.
RUL-008  Regression and boundary tests  [R1 · Must] Maintain at least 80 source-backed scenarios across six sectors, three checked locations, other-state gaps, unknowns and effective-date boundaries. Use a separate AI validation run to challenge expected results. Accept: all critical results and exclusions pass; unresolved interpretations remain held.
RUL-009  Safe updates and scheduling  [R1 · Must] Generate task suggestions from approved results, but require user confirmation of dates and reminders where information is incomplete. Accept: a rule change flags existing tasks for review rather than silently deleting or backdating them.
EXAMPLE OF A SCOPED RESULT  A packaged-food manufacturer may receive a source-backed NAFDAC preparation pathway and questions about its product/facility. A software consultancy must not receive it solely because both are SMEs. The underlying activity-specific rule must pass AI publication gates. [S07]


10 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Workspace, reminders and service help
WRK-001  Business workspace  [R1 · Must] Show profile, saved templates, drafts, purchases, assessment history, tasks and enquiries. Accept: all data survives refresh and device changes; localStorage is limited to non-sensitive display preferences.
WRK-002  Personal task tracking  [R1 · Must] Support not-started, in-progress, completed-by-user and needs-review states, with notes and optional due dates. Accept: a user-completed item is never described as accepted by a regulator.
WRK-003  Reminders  [R1 · Must] Offer opt-in email reminders using Africa/Lagos scheduling, configurable offsets and a delivery log. Accept: duplicate scheduler runs do not duplicate messages; unknown statutory dates cannot become automatic deadlines.
WRK-004  Delivery preferences  [R1 · Must] Separate necessary account/purchase messages from optional reminders and marketing. Accept: unsubscribe and preference changes are enforced before dispatch, not just when the job was created.
WRK-005  Private evidence boundary  [R1 · Must] R1 stores structured task notes and references, not uploaded identity or regulatory evidence files. Accept: upload controls are absent or disabled. Evidence uploads are R2 and require malware scanning, access controls and retention review.
WRK-006  Activity and support  [R1 · Must] Record meaningful user events and offer a support form with a ticket reference. Accept: logs omit draft bodies and unnecessary personal details; support confirms receipt only after the ticket is durably saved.
SRV-001  Service discovery  [R1 · Must] Explain registration, tax, accounting, employment, legal, privacy and sector-approval assistance. Accept: service pages are useful even when no provider is listed and do not imply government affiliation.
SRV-002  Real enquiries  [R1 · Must] At R1, capture the category, scope, contact details and explicit sharing consent; route to Valo’s actual support workflow. Accept: the user sees “Enquiry received”, not “Appointment booked”; failed dispatch is visible to staff.
SRV-003  Verified profiles only  [R1 · Must] Publish providers only after identity, relevant credentials, permission and service descriptions are checked; show the scope/date of verification. Accept: no fictional provider, fabricated rating or unsupported “accredited” claim reaches production.
SRV-004  Future bookings  [R2/R3 · Must] R2 enables quote and availability requests. R3 paid booking requires a separately gated cancellation, refund, dispute and settlement flow. Accept: booking or payment controls remain feature-disabled until that gate is approved.
R1 does not promise provider response times until operational agreements exist. No customer document is sent to a provider without specific consent to the chosen attachment or information set.

11 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Payments, purchases and entitlement
Proposed launch model: one-off premium templates and curated bundles, priced in NGN. A hosted PSP checkout keeps card capture outside Valo. Server verification and webhook processing must follow current Paystack documentation and the signing mode active on Valo’s account. [S16–S17]
PAY-001  Server-priced orders  [R1 · Must] Create immutable order lines from the current approved price book, with tax treatment and total in integer kobo. Accept: a client-modified amount, currency, product or discount cannot alter the charged order.
PAY-002  Hosted checkout  [R1 · Must] Create the PSP session server-side and redirect to its supported hosted flow. Accept: Valo never stores card numbers, security codes or payment credentials; live and test modes use isolated keys and data.
PAY-003  Authoritative confirmation  [R1 · Must] Grant entitlement only after server verification of successful status, merchant transaction reference, order, amount and currency. Accept: a browser success URL alone cannot unlock a template.
PAY-004  Webhook authenticity  [R1 · Must] Validate the raw-body signature with the provider’s supported scheme; apply replay checks where supported. Durably record validated events before acknowledgement. Accept: invalid, replayed and duplicate events cannot create a new purchase.
PAY-005  Atomic fulfilment  [R1 · Must] Process order settlement, entitlement and receipt-outbox creation in one database transaction with uniqueness constraints. Accept: callback and webhook races, retries and worker restarts create one grant only.
PAY-006  Pending and failed payments  [R1 · Must] Show pending, failed, abandoned and verified-success states and provide a safe status refresh. Accept: interrupted browser sessions recover their purchase without requiring an unnecessary second payment.
PAY-007  Reconciliation  [R1 · Must] Run daily transaction reconciliation and alert on unmatched amounts, missing events and fulfilment differences. Accept: finance can resolve a discrepancy with an audit trail and without editing financial history directly.
PAY-008  Refunds and disputes  [R1 · Must] Implement approved refund eligibility, authorisation and PSP status tracking. Accept: a refund is not marked complete when merely requested; entitlement effects are explicit and user-owned drafts are not silently deleted.
PAY-009  Receipts and licence access  [R1 · Must] Issue a Valo purchase receipt, show purchase history and allow re-download under the agreed licence. Accept: a generated business invoice is never confused with proof of payment or an official government tax invoice.
PAY-010  No unapproved billing expansion  [R1 · Must] Keep recurring subscriptions, wallets, split settlements and provider collections off in R1. Accept: later billing products require approved economics, terms, failure handling and their own migration/reconciliation tests.

12 / FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Administration and content operations
ADM-001  Operational console  [R1 · Must] Provide separate content, customer-support, finance and system areas with server-enforced roles. Accept: a finance user cannot read document bodies and an author cannot change payment configuration.
ADM-002  Editorial workflow  [R1 · Must] Use draft → AI-review → validation → eligible → published → superseded/withdrawn, plus held. Accept: retain model/run IDs, findings, evidence, hashes and timestamps. A separate publisher service releases only eligible versions; no per-item human approval is needed and a drafting run cannot approve itself.
ADM-003  Source registry  [R1 · Must] Manage authorities, URLs, jurisdiction, instrument/effective versions, snapshots and next-check dates. Accept: a source change opens AI drafting/review and validation jobs; publication follows only after every gate passes, not directly from a crawler alert.
ADM-004  Rule publishing  [R1 · Must] Validate condition schemas, preview affected profiles and run regression scenarios before publishing a new ruleset. Accept: failed critical tests block release and prior versions can be restored without losing assessment history.
ADM-005  Template release control  [R1 · Must] Require provenance, rights evidence, rendered DOCX/PDF samples, scoped limitations and a passing AI review manifest. Accept: every published item has an immutable eligible version and reproducible export tests; specialist sign-off is not an acceptance dependency.
ADM-006  Commercial configuration  [R1 · Must] Version prices, bundles, quotas and access rules with effective dates and a change log. Accept: historic orders retain their original terms and values after pricing changes.
ADM-007  Safety switches  [R1 · Must] Allow authorised staff to disable an unsafe guide/rule/template, AI generation or checkout independently. Accept: free unaffected guidance remains available and the reason, actor and customer impact are recorded.
ADM-008  Support escalation  [R1 · Must] Track content complaints, payments, privacy requests and provider enquiries separately. Route content issues to the AI reassessment queue and accountable content owner. Accept: anyone can report an error free of charge; unresolved items are held or withdrawn without waiting for an unavailable specialist.
ADM-009  Audit integrity  [R1 · Must] Record sensitive access, publication, role change, refund and recovery actions with actor, object, reason and timestamp. Accept: application users cannot modify audit history; tampering and excessive privileged access generate alerts.
Recommended editorial cadence
Check source availability/changes daily; triage alerts weekly. Re-run substantive AI checks for high-impact tax, employment, privacy and sector content at least monthly, and lower-risk guidance quarterly, or sooner after a change. 14A / AI CONTENT OPERATIONS
AI drafting review and publication
This internal workflow replaces mandatory specialist review for the catalogue, guidance and rule releases. It is separate from the four customer drafting tools. All targets below are Valo operating controls, not a guarantee of legal accuracy. The risk framework in S24 informs the controls; it does not certify this operating model.
EDP-001  Permitted publication scope  [R1 · Must] The product owner sets allowed content classes, jurisdictions, risk levels and blocked activities once at policy level. AI can publish general templates and sourced guidance within that policy. Bespoke legal opinions, guarantees and professional certification are excluded. Accept: publication needs no human signature per item, but cannot expand its own scope.
EDP-002  Evidence collection  [R1 · Must] Retrieve authoritative instruments and current official guidance; retain actual text, title, issuing body, passage, jurisdiction, effective period, retrieval date and hash. News, search snippets and model memory are discovery aids only. Accept: failed retrieval, unclear amendments or missing operative provisions hold affected claims rather than generating substitutes.
EDP-003  AI drafting  [R1 · Must] Draft from the approved evidence bundle and content schema. Map every material legal claim, deadline, rate and applicability condition to a supporting passage; distinguish source statements from interpretation and optional practice. Record missing evidence, assumptions, provenance and template rights. Accept: invented citations, business facts and rights assertions fail validation.
EDP-004  Separate AI checks  [R1 · Must] Run a factual/source check and a separate challenge for scope, omissions, exceptions and misleading certainty, apart from drafting. Reviewers receive the source bundle and candidate, not each other’s verdict. Log model, prompt and run IDs; another model is optional. Accept: unexplained disagreement holds release. Model agreement is not independent professional validation.
EDP-005  Enhanced checks  [R1 · Must] For tax, employment, privacy, governance, contracts and regulated-sector content, check controlling text, amendments, effective dates, exceptions and jurisdiction. Contract templates must expose party/use assumptions and unresolved clauses. Accept: a material uncertainty holds the whole template or inseparable rule; detachable unsupported claims may be removed and the complete item rechecked.
EDP-006  Mechanical validation  [R1 · Must] Validate schemas, source locators, dates, scope, numeric extraction, rights records, required fields and disclosures. Run deterministic arithmetic and rule fixtures; render both export formats. Accept: every mandatory check passes on the exact candidate version. A model’s confidence or pass verdict cannot override a failed deterministic check.

14A / RELEASE AND CORRECTION CONTROLS
Automated release gates and evidence
EDP-007  Publication manifest  [R1 · Must] Bind evidence, separate reviews, risk/scope, rights and test results to immutable content, source, model/prompt and policy versions. The publisher rechecks hashes, status and freshness atomically at release. Accept: edits, expired evidence, failed tests or a changed policy invalidate eligibility and return the item to review; no per-item human approval is required.
EDP-008  Transparent public status  [R1 · Must] Show “AI drafted and reviewed; not reviewed by a Nigerian legal, tax or sector specialist”, source-check date, scope and material limitations on detail pages and exports. Distinguish business drafts from general guidance. Accept: no “lawyer approved”, “professionally verified”, “guaranteed compliant” or equivalent claim without actual supporting review.
EDP-009  Least privilege automation  [R1 · Must] Drafters/reviewers cannot publish or alter policy. A bounded publisher service may release only eligible manifests; it cannot access private customer drafts, change prices, send filings or move money. Retrieval uses allowlisted official sources and isolates downloaded text as untrusted data. Accept: injection, forged manifests and direct publish attempts fail and are logged.
EDP-010  Editorial evaluation  [R1 · Must] Maintain 60 editorial cases separate from 60 customer AI cases and 80 rule scenarios. Include fake/misquoted citations, conflicting dates, missing text, omitted exceptions, wrong jurisdiction, unsafe clauses, injection and stale sources. Expected publish/hold outcomes need source excerpts or deterministic failure conditions. Accept: zero critical false publications and at least 95% overall; no claim of a legal-accuracy percentage.
EDP-011  Monitoring and correction  [R1 · Must] Check sources daily and re-review high-impact content monthly, other content quarterly, or immediately after material change/error. Suspend affected rules, stop new statutory reminders, flag old assessments/tasks and re-run all gates. Log the change and notify affected users when material. Accept: withdrawal reaches search, caches, downloads and checker; past customer files are never silently rewritten.
EDP-012  Holds and accountability  [R1 · Must] Missing evidence, conflicting reviews or provider failure leaves an item held; elapsed time never clears it. Retry with new evidence/configuration or narrow and recheck scope; otherwise keep it unavailable and show an official route. The content owner monitors queues, rights and corrections; specialist review is optional when later available, not a launch dependency. Accept: owners cannot waive a failed mandatory gate.
Publication decisions: all gates pass → publish automatically; removable unsupported claim → revise and rerun; material ambiguity or failed gate → hold; credible defect in live content → suspend, correct and rerun. AI service failure pauses new releases while unaffected current content remains available only within its freshness window.

Response and failure conventions
Use JSON schema validation on requests and responses. Errors contain code, safe message, requestId and field-level details; never stack traces or secrets. Use 401 for missing identity, 403 for forbidden operations, 404 where existence should be concealed, 409 for revision conflicts, 422 for validation and 429 for quotas. Return a Retry-After hint where meaningful.
Payment fulfilment transaction
Validate event → persist the event uniquely → acknowledge → verify payment server-side → lock the order → compare amount/currency/reference → settle order and grant entitlement once → append receipt notification to the outbox → commit → dispatch notification. The callback uses the same idempotent fulfilment service. Refund/reversal events enter an audited compensating workflow, not a destructive rewrite. [S16–S17]
Document export transaction
Authorise → validate saved revision → insert/find unique export job → render from the immutable revision → write private object → atomically mark ready with checksum → send ready notification. Failed object writes never expose a “completed” export. Reconciliation removes orphaned temporary objects and retries safe failures.

NFR-005 / Recovery | Database RPO ≤1h and core-service RTO ≤4h; regenerable export objects RPO ≤24h. | Restore into isolated environment, verify data/permissions and replay reconciliation; provider backup capability proved.
NFR-006 / Accessibility | WCAG 2.2 AA target across critical flows; keyboard, visible focus, screen-reader labels, errors and reflow. | Automated checks plus manual keyboard and assistive-technology review. [S20]
NFR-007 / Browsers and mobile | Latest and preceding major Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari at release; Android Chrome/iOS Safari; no horizontal page overflow at 360px. | Document supported-version matrix, real-device checks and touch interaction tests.
NFR-008 / Localisation | English at launch; NGN formatting; Africa/Lagos user-facing deadlines; UTC event storage and unambiguous dates. | Timezone-boundary and naira/export glyph tests; unknown dates remain unset.
NFR-009 / Reliability | No silent draft loss, duplicated entitlement or unrecorded critical state transition; bounded retriable jobs. | Concurrency, network-loss, webhook-race, worker-crash and database-failure tests.
NFR-010 / Operability | All critical journeys instrumented; alerts have owners and runbooks; no private document text in logs. | Synthetic failures verify alerts, redaction and support escalation.
NFR-011 / Maintainability | Typed contracts, lint/type checks, reviewed migrations and architectural boundaries; 80% branch coverage in payment/auth/rules modules. | CI report plus scenario tests; coverage percentage never substitutes for negative security cases.

Cold starts and actual Nigerian access latency must be included in user-facing performance reviews. If Replit or a selected provider cannot meet recovery, privacy or export targets, change the relevant infrastructure before launch rather than silently lowering the target.

Test layers and ownership
Engineers own unit, contract, integration and concurrency tests. QA owns journeys, regressions and fixture provenance. The AI pipeline proposes and challenges content/rules; the content owner monitors evidence and held items without providing legal sign-off. Technical security/privacy assessment remains separate. The product owner accepts usability, scope and operating policy.
Release-blocking defects
Any cross-tenant exposure, incorrect entitlement grant, unverified payment unlock, uncontrolled secret exposure, silent draft overwrite, false verified legal claim, critical/high exploitable vulnerability or failed restore drill blocks R1. Cosmetic and minor usability defects require named owners and a dated fix plan; they must not disguise missing promised functionality.
EVIDENCE PACK  Every Must requirement links to a ticket, code/content revision, test or review result, owner and release. Keep a traceability register in the repository and refresh it at each gate. Do not mark deferred R2/R3 work as completed R1 functionality.


24 / OPERATING THE LIVE PRODUCT
Runbooks and launch readiness
Minimum runbook set
RUNBOOK | REQUIRED ACTIONS AND OWNER
Unavailable site / database | Engineering lead: detect, confirm impact, freeze risky changes, communicate, restore/rollback and validate critical journeys.
Payments or entitlement failure | Finance + engineering: reconcile PSP status, prevent duplicate collection, repair once through audited workflow, notify the affected buyer.
Broken export / AI outage | Engineering: isolate the failing feature, preserve draft, retry safely, provide manual/template fallback and assess cost impact.
Incorrect compliance content | Content owner + pipeline: suspend affected versions, recheck sources, identify impacted results, rerun gates, publish correction and notify when material.
Security or privacy incident | Incident lead + privacy owner: contain, revoke access, preserve evidence, assess source-backed notice requirements and record remediation.
Provider enquiry dispute | Support: acknowledge, check sharing consent and routing, set expectations and escalate; do not claim a booking that was never confirmed.
Deletion and recovery | Engineering + privacy owner: enforce retention, track legal holds, verify external deletion and reapply deletion events after a restore.

Commercial launch checklist
Approve business identity, domain ownership, PSP onboarding, prices, tax treatment, licences, terms/refunds and privacy notice. Use source-grounded AI checks for legal/tax wording; the product and finance/privacy owners accept their operational decisions. No specialist signature is a launch prerequisite. Resolve material evidence gaps or disable the affected activity before launch.
Confirm all R1 Must requirements and content floors; pass payment, tenant-isolation, accessibility and restore gates; perform a controlled live purchase/refund; verify transactional email authentication/delivery; confirm backup monitoring and staff MFA. Remove demo seeds, test credentials, “coming soon” primary CTAs, fake metrics and placeholder provider records.
Deployment sequence
Freeze scope → verify recoverable backup → approve migration/rollback → deploy staging candidate → obtain product/security operational sign-offs and passing AI content manifest → deploy approved build → run smoke tests → open limited traffic → monitor → expand only with healthy service and support. Subsequent eligible content updates publish automatically.
After release
Run daily payment reconciliation and error review; triage new content reports promptly. Review usage, support burden, source freshness, AI cost, conversion and retention weekly. Test a restore at least quarterly and after a major data/infrastructure change. Maintain a risk register and publish corrections without erasing historical evidence.
Business-hours support is the initial proposed staffing model; urgent security/payment monitoring still needs a named responder. No 24/7 human-support claim should appear unless it is actually staffed and funded.
